I've loved spaghetti bolognese since I was a kid. Most of the time it was served to me from a ready-made instant sauce, but once I grew into cooking I managed to refine the recipe to a decent standard.

## Ingredients

Onion, garlic, minced meat, tomato passata, carrot,
and the seasoning: salt, pepper, oregano, basil.

I'm deliberately not giving proportions. Everything by eye.

## The recipe

1. I fry the finely chopped onion, then add the minced meat: chicken, pork, beef. The last one gives a nice, dark colour. I forgot to mention that just before adding the meat I throw finely chopped garlic into the pan and fry it briefly. Sometimes, instead of chopping, I cut the garlic into the thinnest slices I can manage, the way it's done in "Goodfellas".

   [The scene from "Goodfellas"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rQV6CijIzrc)

2. I pour in the tomato passata and add finely grated carrot, which does the heavy lifting. Many recipes recommend sweetening tomato sauce. In this case the carrot replaces sugar for me.

3. I season it: usually salt, pepper, basil, oregano. I simmer it on low heat, reducing the tomato sauce. The whole process can take about an hour, depending on how much passata went in.
